# eTMF Quality Control Workflow

## Purpose

The QC workflow defines how controlled trial documents are reviewed before they become inspection-ready.

## QC Process

```text
Document Created
       ↓
Metadata Review
       ↓
Document QC
       ↓
QC Decision
   ↙       ↘
FAIL       PASS
 ↓          ↓
Correction  Approval Review
 ↓          ↓
Re-QC      Approved
            ↓
         Effective
            ↓
          Filed
