# Inspection Readiness

## Objective

The simulated eTMF is designed around the principle that essential trial documentation should remain complete, consistent, traceable, and readily retrievable throughout the study lifecycle.

## Inspection Readiness Criteria

A document should be considered inspection-ready when:

- The correct document is available
- The document belongs to the correct study
- The artifact classification is appropriate
- Version information is controlled
- Required QC has been completed
- Required approval has been completed
- Effective dates are defined where applicable
- Filing location is correct
- Metadata is complete
- The document can be retrieved efficiently

## Traceability

The Master Register acts as the central index connecting document information with its controlled filing location.
